## 🚀 About

**PeerShare** is a real-time platform designed to make sharing **text, notes, and code** simple and fast.

Instead of sending screenshots, opening a messaging app, copying things between devices, or sending unnecessary files, PeerShare provides a simple workspace where people can collaborate using a **room**.

> **Create a room → Share the code → Join → Start sharing.**

The project is mainly focused on students, developers, and people who frequently need to quickly share small pieces of information.

---

## 🎯 Why PeerShare?

Sharing a small piece of code or text shouldn't require a complicated process.

Imagine you're sitting with a classmate and want to share a piece of code.

Instead of:

```text
Copy
   ↓
Open Messenger / WhatsApp / Email
   ↓
Send
   ↓
Open on another device
   ↓
Copy again
```

PeerShare aims to make it:

```text
Create Room
     ↓
Share Room Code
     ↓
Join Room
     ↓
Share Instantly ⚡
```

Simple.

Fast.

Useful.

## 🔐 Authentication

PeerShare will use a simple **passwordless authentication system**.

```text
Enter Email
     ↓
Receive OTP
     ↓
Enter OTP
     ↓
Verify
     ↓
You're In ✓
```

No password management.

No complicated registration process.
